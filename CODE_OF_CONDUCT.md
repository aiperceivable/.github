# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a positive experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Personal attacks or derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission

Technical disagreement is not misconduct. Arguing that a design is wrong, that a
conformance fixture encodes the wrong behavior, or that a guarantee does not hold is
welcome and useful — it is how a specification improves. What is out of bounds is directing
that criticism at a person rather than at the work.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — repositories, issues, pull
requests, and discussions under the
[aiperceivable](https://github.com/aiperceivable) organization — and in public spaces
when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at
**team@aiperceivable.org**.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is
deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers are obligated to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
